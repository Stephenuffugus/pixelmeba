**Recyclers (B04) run out of food and go extinct in 3 of 6 seeds.**
- Their debris patch goes from 12.25 C to 1.95 C (mean) by 60 s and 0.01 C by 120 s.
- At 120 s their intake is 0.0004 C/s, and they record `FOOD_ACCESS_LOW` with mean value 0.00

- Their mean position stays at (66–67, 48–49) through 300 s. They sense food only 2 cells away

- Pooled over seeds there are 62 Recycler deaths, all `DEATH_STARVATION`.
- The Recyclers went extinct at 542, 565 and 585 s on seeds 314159, 130363 and 155921. The other
  three seeds hold 1–2 Recyclers each.
- Two seeds never exceeded the 8 founders (peak 8 at 1 s), and first Recycler divisions range
  from 54.9 to 463.4 s.

**Crumbsmiths (B06) are caught in an energy trap on their starch.**
- They start at E 50 and pay about 0.4 E/s of maintenance plus 0.4 E/s while secreting. At 15 s
  they take in 0.0098 C/s, which is about 0.29 E/s at 30 E per C.
- Mean E falls to 39.7 at 15 s and 30.8 at 60 s, below the 35 E secretion threshold. At 60 s,
  99 % record `SECRETION_ENERGY_LOW`.
- Conversion then almost stops. Carbon in the starch disk goes 46.63 → 40.45 C between 15 s and
  60 s, then only 40.45 → 37.90 C from 60 s to 600 s. Total conversion by 600 s is 9.78–12.99 C
  of 48.6.
- The Crumbsmiths drift toward the Sunbead colony, with mean position (59.6, 51.9) at 120 s.
  `SECRETION_NO_SUBSTRATE` rises to 22–40 % of samples.
- They persist (9–13 alive at 600 s) but divide rarely: 2–7 divisions per seed. Their first
  divisions range from 138.7 to 365.5 s (median 193.0 s).
- At 300–600 s, 95–97 % of living Crumbsmiths are held by `DIV_BLOCK_ENERGY`.

## Proposed recipe revisions

**Proposals only. No recipe was changed.**
- The D06 targets are met on 6/6 seeds, so P1.12 does not require a FIRST_DISH_V2.
- The proposals below address the ecology the guide text promises: "Crumbsmiths release enzyme
  onto the starch patch; Recyclers eat the debris patch".
- Extinction and a quiet corner are valid outcomes. None of these is a rescue.
- Following CONTENT_TABLES §11, each proposal changes one recipe setting (proposals 2 and 3 each
  edit two coupled numbers, for the reason given), lives in its own new
  revision (FIRST_DISH_V1 is kept), and is rerun with this tool on all six seeds before any
  choice is made.
- No mechanics change is proposed.

1. **Starch patch sugar bootstrap: 0 → 0.10 sugar C per cell** in the (66,64) r 5 patch. That
   adds 8.1 C over its 81 cells, the same bootstrap EXP_A uses.
   - *Why*: the Crumbsmiths' secretion stalls within about 60 s because their energy falls below
     the 35 E emit threshold (99 % `SECRETION_ENERGY_LOW` at 60 s). After that, conversion
     almost stops (≈ 2.5 C converted in the remaining 540 s).
   - A small early energy source should keep them secreting long enough for the enzyme-derived
     sugar to carry them.
   - *Watch*: sugar spreads fast (the sugar patch lost 94 % of its disk carbon in 15 s), so part
     of the bootstrap will diffuse away or reach Sprinters.
   - Bootstrap sugar is a logged recipe input, so enzyme-derived sugar stays separately
     attributable.
   - *Measure*: B06 `SECRETING` share at 60/120 s, starch converted by 600 s, B06 divisions and
     first-division spread.
2. **Debris patch amount: 0.25 → 0.50 C per cell**, with bound N 0.025 → 0.05 so the C:N ratio is
   unchanged.
   - *Why*: the Recyclers empty 12.25 C by about 120 s and then starve. The patch disk lost
     ≈ 0.29 C/s over the first 15 s and ≈ 0.13 C/s from 15 to 60 s. The debris disk held only
     1.01 C at 300 s; Sunbead detritus does not build up there until about 600 s.
   - *Expectation*: doubling probably buys on the order of 100 s, not 500 s. That is not enough
     to bridge to the Sunbead detritus on its own. It is listed because it is the smallest
     single-number change, and the measurement would show whether it suffices.
3. **Alternative to 2: move the debris patch (and the B04 founders' centre with it) from (66,48)
   to (58,48)**. This is one placement decision that edits two coupled recipe fields: the debris
   field patch centre and the B04 founder group centre. Moving one without the other would put the
   Recyclers away from their food.
   - *Why*: Sunbead detritus builds up inside the colony. *Spot check (seed 130363)*: the
     detritus centroid was (51.3, 49.5) at 300 s, with 65 C within r 12 of (48,48), and the
     Sunbeads spanned x 44–62. A Recycler group placed at the colony's edge would reach that
     detritus sooner than one at (66,48).
   - That would close the food web the guide describes (Sunbead → detritus → Recycler). It would
     also return some bound nutrient to the Sunbeads, who are `NUTRIENT_LIMITED`.
   - *Watch*: in the same spot check, the Sunbead founders occupied x 49–53, so a r 4 patch at
     (58,48) (x 54–62) sits about one cell east of them. Check founder placement with the recipe
     realizer, and check Sunbead crowding over the patch. Whether Recyclers survive the gap
     between about 120 s and the colony's arrival is an open question that the rerun must
     answer.
4. **No change proposed** for the sugar patch, Sprinters or Sunbeads.
   - Sprinter intake and division already meet the targets on every seed.
   - The Sunbead boom and turnover is honest nutrient limitation, with carbon supplied from the
     air.
   - If a later review finds the Sunbead-only first division at 29.3 s misleading for the
     tutorial ("See a Sprinter split" goal), that is a presentation question, not a recipe one.
