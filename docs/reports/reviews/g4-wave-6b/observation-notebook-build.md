## PROPOSED DECISIONS
1. **Snapshot limits are per device** (20 shelf records of kind 'snapshot' across all dishes; D02 "Snapshot bookmarks count against storage limits"); the 50 bookmark limit is per dish.
2. **Deleting a bookmark keeps its snapshot** on the shelf (the same bookmark exists in other copies of the dish's save: slot, previous copy, Continue); the confirmation says so and Storage removes it. Opening a bookmark whose snapshot is gone shows it as text only; the notebook entry itself is never rewritten by a read.
3. **"Export without names or notes"** replaces bookmark titles with "Bookmark at m:ss" and drops snapshot references (device storage); summaries (generated from recorded events and counts) stay. A normal export keeps the references; on another device they read as removed.
4. **Gallery picture = a crop of sprite rows, not pixels** (16 × 16 cells, ≤ 200 sprites, one ground byte per cell; deposits are not stored), re-drawn at 14 px per cell (sprites ×2). Worst case notebook 582 KB < 1 MB. Bookmarks and gallery entries survive Undo like journal notes, so on load only their species are checked against the dish (not ticks or birth ids, which a later Undo can legitimately exceed); the worker checks birth id and tick at save time.
5. **The Bookmark sheet pauses the dish** (the bookmarked moment is the one on screen) and restores the prior speed on Close/Esc, as the Share sheet does; it blocks dish keys while open.
6. **Event-row bookmarks are text only** and record the event id found in the ring by the row's first event (tick, type, species, cause); when the ring no longer holds it, eventId is null and the summary omits "(event N)".
