## D-01xx · 2026-10-1x · Phase 5 wave 14 · Wave 14 scope and the task writer's rulings (habitat geometry, region pins, habitat communities, New Dish habitats; then the habitat enable)
Context: the wave 14 task writer (run wf_f7ade737-dbd; docs/agent/g5-wave-14.tasks.js.txt; docs/reports/reviews/g5-wave-14/tasks-writer.md), lead-reviewed against BUILD_DIRECTIVE P5.4, CT §8.2, ARCH §4 and §11, UX New Dish, D-0106, D-0111, D-0121 and D-0122. Every PROPOSED DECISION of the writer (1–21) is accepted; a builder may still report evidence against a ruling.
Decision:
- **Scope.** Two stages: stage 1 habitat-geometry (4271, critical 'own') and region-pins (4272, not critical; moved from p5.4b against D-0122's placement because the pins need only the regions schema that habitat-geometry writes first, and stage 2 then keeps protocol.ts and host.ts for habitat-communities); stage 2 habitat-communities (4271, critical 'own') and new-dish-habitats (4272, not critical). verifyGroups [['habitat-geometry', 'habitat-communities'], ['region-pins', 'new-dish-habitats']]: rules verifiers 4390 and 4391, saves verifiers 4396 and 4397, re-verify 4394; 4398 stays free; never 4290.
- **Schema.** New optional HabitatSchema arrays without defaults (D-0111's pattern, not new geometry-op kinds as the briefs' touchpoint 244 proposed): furnish (S05, S06, S11, S12 over a disk, rect or perimeter), objects (M10, M11 at a cell), regions (≤ 6: id, label, disk or rect, anchor), suggestedCommunity (species groups, region, count 5, radius 10, optional patches); RecipeSchema.community. A perimeter is the disk's in-dish cells with a 4-neighbour outside it (H11: 88 cells). habitatRules.ts imports neither registry.ts nor recipes.ts.
- **Realization.** Placed-structure ids run habitat edges, habitat devices, habitat furnish areas, recipe edges, recipe devices (a habitat without furnish areas gets today's ids). Habitat food objects are logged external inputs `habitat:<id>:<material>` placed after initializeLedger, in both starts. withHabitatOverride also drops the recipe's lid, light mode, drying, baseLight, ops, removeStones, edges and devices (FIRST_DISH_V1 records them explicitly, so a habitat start would otherwise inherit them); the v1.0 starts stay byte-identical (step 0 golden).
- **Habitat content.** Ids H04_LEAF_POOL … H11_GRAZING_MEADOW (never renamed). H09's wick is habitat geometry (D-0106); H10's wall is 114 WALL cells with six S01 on the west edges of (64,61)–(64,66) (D-0106); H10's starch is a community patch (E209's numbers: 0.50 per cell, radius 6, at (55,64)). Regions and community centres for every habitat are tabled in STATE (CT leaves H04, H07, H08, H11 open; H07 and H11 grazers start apart from their food). Numbers CT leaves open take the Water Garden values (D02's rule).
- **Suggested Community.** Eligible cells: the radius-10 disk around the region anchor ∩ the region ∩ the dish ∩ canOccupy, minus used cells; each group takes the first five of a permutation on the new stream STREAMS.communityPlace; a group with too few cells is refused, never cropped; organisms enter through introduceOrganism logged `introduce:community:<id>`. The choice is a recorded New Dish override (`community: true`, requires `empty`), kept in the provenance and in recipe files, so What if?, compare starts and recipe files rebuild the same dish.
- **Offer rules.** One module (habitatOfferProblem, communityOfferProblem): phase, structures, systems, materials, species; the validator refuses a shipped habitat that fails them (closing the missing phase check on enabledHabitats). The community realization check runs in tools/content-validate.ts and the tests, not in validateContent (which runs at the worker's boot).
- **Region pins** numbered 1–6 (probes use A–F), static and procedural (no atlas change), below probes and above links, read from the world's recorded habitat (old dishes show none), a text legend in the probe panel; pins do not count against the six probe regions.
- **New Dish.** The three v1.0 starts keep their ids, test ids, labels and notes; new habitats are `habitat:<ID>`, ordered by phase then id; Empty is the default and resets when the start changes; the preview says "It places: …" plus the lid, light and drying line.
- **Versions.** No world schema or protocol bump (every addition optional). contentHash moves at each stage (new content files) and at the enable; trajectory digests ignore it.
- **The habitat enable** (lead, after the wave): enabledHabitats + H04–H11; content:validate --write; the fence unchanged; three fence dishes added with --add-file from the drafts (FENCE_H10_GATE_CYCLE: edges and climate together; FENCE_H09_DRYING_COMMUNITY; FENCE_H06_DUSK_COMMUNITY), since no shipped recipe uses H04–H11 before wave 15; tests/tools/listing.test.ts re-pinned to habitats of phase ≤ 4 (the v1.1 listing is p5.8b's); P5.4 ticked against its Build line (BUILD_DIRECTIVE P5.4 has no "Done when").
- **Section references** corrected: habitats are CT §8.2 (CT §14 is limits); the content schema is ARCH §4.
Reason: optional fields keep every v1.0 and v1.1 record and save valid; a recorded community choice and its own RNG stream keep re-runs, What if? and replays equal; nothing ships before the enable, so the fence holds while the habitats are built.
