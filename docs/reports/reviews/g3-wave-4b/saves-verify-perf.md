## Problems (most severe first)

- MINOR [perf] src/sim/transport.ts:199 — filmCoefficients now clears `derived.fieldActive[film]` on a live world, but a reloaded world rebuilds every flag as 1 (world.ts:191; deserializeWorld does not restore it). After the first tick of any dish with no film, the live world and its reload differ in this derived flag. That contradicts the stated contract in applyNow (commands.ts:94-95): "leave derived state exactly as a reload would rebuild it". The flag is unhashed and, as measured, does not change outcomes. The coefficients are identical when film is all zero. All current film writers call markField: construction.ts:143, sample.ts:332/476, tools.ts:99, structures.ts:387, commands.ts deposit. However, nothing guards a future film writer that forgets markField; such a writer would silently halve no edges and change results. Suggested fix: assert `inactiveFieldsNonZero(world)` is empty in a film fixture after construction and after sample transfer, or have deserializeWorld derive the flag from content. — Repro: `node --import tsx tmp/verify-saves-0/probe.ts full 800` reports flags(live/ser/file)=0/1/1 at ticks 1, 2, 36 and 400 on every recipe, and every continuation still matches.
- MINOR [perf] src/sim/movement.ts:184-203 — the module-level `FOOD_ARRS` and `foodStarch/foodOil/foodProtein` keep the last decided world's field arrays reachable after that world is discarded. intake.ts clears its equivalent `fieldAt` in clearScratch (intake.ts:487); movement.ts has no equivalent. This affects memory only (a few 128 KB arrays of one dead world, for example after a comparison or a reload). It does not affect determinism: the arrays are re-picked at every decide() call (verified with per-tick interleaving below). — Repro: reading the code; no behavioural effect.
