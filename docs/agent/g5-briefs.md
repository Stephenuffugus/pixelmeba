### Requirements
- **C1** Day/night: settings lightMode 'cycle' with a 240 s period: day 90 s at factor 1.0, dusk 30 s linear 1.0→0.05, night 90 s at 0.05, dawn 30 s linear 0.05→1.0. The phase is saved and a snapshot restores it. A manual light change is a command. The inspector shows the phase and every light factor. _(SPEC §4.6, §4.4, §12.1; CT §12.2)_
- **C2** Light formula: effective = clamp(clamp(base×cycle [+lamps, Phase 6],0,1) × paintedShade × roofShade(S11 0.25) × canopy, 0,1), computed per tick in stage 2. Signal glow adds 0 light. With no cycle, roof or canopy it reduces exactly to the current base×shade. _(SPEC §4.4; CR R19; CT §12.2)_
- **C3** Local warmth, 0–1 index per cell. Each tick it relaxes 1 % toward the ambient value (settings.warmth, default 0.50) and diffuses at 0.05 across passable edges. S09/S10 add ±0.01/s while On, and overlapping neighbours add. Clamp stored and shown values. No material is created. Suitability reads local warmth instead of settings.warmth. _(SPEC §4.7, §6.3; CT §12.2, §13)_
- **C4** Moisture index: water is fixed at 1.0; gel and sediment start at 0.8. With drying on, uncovered gel/sediment lose 0.001/s down to 0.1. The wetting brush sets 0.9 and dilutes nothing. S11 stops drying on its cell. S12 relaxes adjacent gel/sediment toward 0.8 at 0.01/s while the wick touches water. The shoulder is 0.2, below the range only (already in suitability.ts). The guide states that drying never concentrates chemicals. _(SPEC §4.8, §6.3; CT §12.2, §12.7 (dormancy trigger reads it))_
- **C5** Canopy factor = 1/(1 + Σ A05 biomass/10) over the cell and its 4 neighbours, from start-of-tick biomass, with colonies summed. It applies to every photosynthesizer there, A05 included. Key it on the CANOPY ability, not on the species ID, because A05 is enabled in P5.3. _(SPEC §4.10; CT §12.2)_
- **C6** Stage 2 order: cycle phase → warmth and moisture update → canopy → cache release (Phase 7, no-op) → barrier-aware transport of dissolved, viral and activity fields and companions → S04 directed transfers → gas exchange → decays → resin → neutralization → derived pH, light and moisture. _(SPEC §3.2 stage 2)_
- **C7** New Dish Environment group: lid, light mode (Fixed or Cycle), drying. Recipes and habitats override. Record these as RecipeOverridesRecord fields so What if? and replays realize the dish identically. _(UX §2.3; BUILD_DIRECTIVE P5.1; D02 §8 Controls (source; canonical is silent on defaults))_
- **S1** Edge barriers S01–S04 sit on edges (cap 512, two on one edge is invalid). Class factors: S01 25 % for Dissolved/Activity, blocks Viral and all organisms. S02 50 % for Dissolved/Activity/Viral, Small passes, everything else blocked. S03 Open = no barrier, Closed blocks everything. Diffusion multiplies the edge coefficient by the class factor and companions move in the same fraction. Lamp light passes S01/S02 (Phase 6). _(SPEC §2.4, §4.1, §4.9; CT §4, §5.3, §14)_
- **S2** Transport classes: Dissolved, Activity, Viral, Small, Medium, Large, Fixed, assigned by species record transportClass plus state overrides (attached = Fixed; later: resting F04 daughter Small, B02 disperser Small, free P06 Medium). Organism paths check every crossed edge, with no tunnelling. A host and its attached parasite move as one unit in the host's class (parasites.ts followHosts already copies the host's position). _(SPEC §4.9, §6.4; CT §1.1, §5.3)_
- **S3** S04 one-way channel runs between adjacent water cells. Per second it requests 2 % of every eligible dissolved and viral pool of the source cell × dt, all read from one snapshot, summed and capped per pool, with companions in the same fraction. It also moves one eligible free Small organism per second, chosen deterministically, with exact state kept and no extra actions. No multi-hop in one tick. Deposits, attached life, film and objects never move this way. _(SPEC §4.9; CT §4, §12.2)_
- **S4** Furnishings and devices, one per cell, cap 256: S05 shelter (Small free organisms pass; Medium and Large blocked; solutes and viruses normal), S06 mesh (attachment for B02, B13, fungi and P06; SURF_MESH), S07 basket (holds one food object; prevents partial sampling; removing it leaves the object), S08 resin (removes selected inhibitors/RIVAL in its own cell at ≤ 0.05/s proportionally until 5 units are bound; saturated resin is inert; removal exports the bound activity as a logged export), S09/S10 heater and cooler, S11 roof, S12 wick. Devices start Off. Moving a device is a paused atomic relocation that keeps ID and contents. _(SPEC §2.4, §4.9, §10.4; CT §4)_
- **S5** Removing a structure restores the previous substrate and removes no resources. Erase Structure covers edges and devices. _(SPEC §2.4, §10.4)_
- **S6** Gate preview: selecting a gate shows what can pass, from the exact class table. The tracer animation is cosmetic, runs only while paused, moves nothing and draws no sim randomness. _(SPEC §4.9 last line, §10.8, §15; D02 §10 UI feedback and §20 Transfer preview (source))_
- **S7** Region charts with measured exchange totals across region boundaries, so isolation can be told apart from ecological resistance. Up to 6 regions. Regions are observation masks only. _(SPEC §12.4, §12.8; CR R34; D02 §10, §20 (source); existing src/worker/probes.ts)_

### Risks
- transport.ts is shared by climate-stage2, barrier-transport and the P5.3 signal/RIVAL work. Sequence its owners across waves and never give it to two builders in one wave.
- movement.ts, contacts.ts, births.ts and suitability.ts are also edited by the P5.3/P5.5 organism slices (P07, A04, P06, P10, F04 resting daughters, B12 and P08 moisture dormancy). The lead must assign them per wave or split the edits into helper files.
- windowClear fast path (movement.ts:244): if barrier edges are not marked, decisions will skip traces that should be blocked. This is a silent isolation leak. Fixture: a trace through S01 must be refused even when no blocked cells are nearby.
- Derived light and canopy outside stage 2: updateDerived runs on load and after paused commands with world.tick = T, while an uninterrupted run computed the stage 2 value of tick T−1. All sim readers run after stage 2, so the hash is safe, but the inspector or snapshot could show a different value after reload. Pick one convention (the last completed stage 2) and test it.
- Lazy climate arrays: allocation must be triggered only by commands or settings, never by observation, and must be identical on replay, reload and 4×. The moment of allocation must not depend on paused-versus-queued paths.
- Floating-point identity: barrier factors must multiply only barrier-edge coefficients, and min(ki,kj) must keep its current order. Any refactor of diffuseField breaks the fence.
- Gate tallies: computing per-edge flux inside the kernel would slow every tick. Do it in a separate pass over the barrier edge list (≤ 512) reading pre-step values.
- S04 one-Small-per-second state (nextOrganismTick) is simulation state, so it must be saved and hashed. A cosmetic-only timer would desync replays.
- Contacts and placement across barriers are not settled in the canonical docs. Without a rule, predators eat through membranes and daughters are born on the far side of a membrane, which breaks E209 'no organisms cross'.
- Probes: activities and the S04 channel stay inside stage 2. Resin removes activity in stage 2, so activity boundary totals are not valid where resin sits.
- Perf: per-tick light recompute (16k cells) when the cycle is on, plus warmth diffusion. Measure with tools/sim-run --perf and keep the fast path when no factor changes.

### Open questions
- **Q:** Does S04 also let ordinary diffusion and movement across its edge, or only the directed transfer? **Options:** (a) the edge behaves like a closed shutter except for the directed transfer; (b) ordinary diffusion continues both ways and the channel adds directed flow **Recommendation:** (a). It is honestly one-way, matches CT §4 'adds budgeted directed transport' without a bidirectional leak, and keeps E-tests simple. Log a DECISIONS entry and flag it for the owner.
- **Q:** Can contacts (predation claims, parasite attachment, E12/E16 links) and daughter or fungal placement cross a barrier edge? **Options:** (a) refused when the edge between the two cells blocks either party's class; (b) unrestricted **Recommendation:** (a). Use the same edgePermits check on the segment between the two positions (SPEC §4.9 'free life cannot attach inside a barrier edge'; E209 'no organisms cross'). It costs nothing when no edges exist.
- **Q:** Do edge barriers attenuate warmth diffusion? **Options:** (a) only a closed shutter, walls and stone stop warmth; S01/S02 pass it fully; (b) apply the Dissolved factor **Recommendation:** (a). SPEC §4.7 says 'passable edges' and warmth is an index, not a solute. D02 says 'blocked edges stop local transport'.
- **Q:** Default light mode for New Dish: D02 §8 says the expanded sandbox enables day/night by default, while CT §8.1 presets say Fixed light. **Options:** Cycle default vs Fixed default **Recommendation:** Fixed default (canonical CT §8.1), with Cycle as a New Dish choice. Recipes H06, H08 and H11 and E218 turn it on explicitly.
- **Q:** Where does the cycle phase start, and how is it saved? **Options:** settings.cycleStartTick (phase = (tick − start) mod 2400, starting at day) vs a saved phase counter **Recommendation:** settings.cycleStartTick, present only in cycle mode. It is deterministic, survives snapshot and undo through tick, and is hash-neutral for old worlds. A setLightMode command records it.
- **Q:** Wick 'touches water' and 'adjacent' semantics. **Options:** 4-neighbour vs 8-neighbour **Recommendation:** The wick cell or one of its 4 neighbours is water; it relaxes its 4-neighbour gel/sediment cells. This is consistent with the 4-neighbour transport topology.
- **Q:** How do players draw edge barriers (UX is silent)? **Options:** stroke snapped to nearest cell edges vs tap an edge **Recommendation:** Tap or drag snapped to the nearest edge, with a preview showing refusals (rim, wall, existing barrier, S04 not water↔water) before commit. Keep the brush radius at 1 edge.
