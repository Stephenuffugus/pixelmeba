### Requirements
- **R1** LAB01 is a cell device on one valid water, gel or sediment cell (one device per cell, 256 cap shared with S05–S12). It starts Off. It stores one recipe: Sugar with 0–0.10 N per C (default 0.10), or Broth at 0.10 (needs the broth/brothN fields, which belong to the 'enzymes' system). Capacity 20 C. Rates are 0.01/0.02/0.05 C/s (default 0.02). _(SPEC §11.1, §2.4; CT §4 LAB01, §12.9; D07 §05)_
- **R2** Loading is an external ledger INPUT into the reservoir, and partial fills are allowed. A different recipe or ratio is rejected while any stock remains. Emptying, or removing the device, EXPORTS the exact remainder. Moving keeps the id and contents. The host enforces 'load while paused'; stage 1 accepts the command whenever it is due. _(SPEC §11.1, §2.4 (removing a device exports its inventory); D07 §04, §05, §15)_
- **R3** Stage 3, after releaseFoodObjects and before grit and enzymes: each Running reservoir, in world.devices (cell) order, moves c = min(rate×dt, stockC) into its cell's sugar or broth. It moves n = stockN × c/stockC into sugarN or brothN; the last transfer moves the exact remainders (reuse OBJECT_DUST_C). Both pools commit together, stock is reduced once and the fields are marked. The product is edible in stage 6 of the same tick. An invalid cell (outside the dish or a structure on it) gives output 0 and state Blocked, and the stock stays. No burst is owed for Blocked, Off or paused time. _(SPEC §3.2 row 3, §5.1, §11.1; D07 §05)_
- **R4** The displayed state is derived, never stored: Off, Running, Empty, Blocked or Controller paused. The panel shows stored C and N, rate, output in the last second, cumulative output, and 'active dispensing time' = stockC/rate. The record saves cumulative output. _(SPEC §11.1; D07 §04, §15)_
- **R5** LAB02 is a cell device, Off by default, with intensities 0.10/0.25/0.50 (default 0.25). It lights every cell within four-neighbour PATH distance d ≤ 3 from the lamp cell (d = 0 included), adding intensity×(1−d/4). Walls and closed shutters block the path; S01 and S02 transmit; furnishings are ignored. It does not warm, draws no organism by fiat, and holds no material. _(SPEC §11.2; CT §12.9; D07 §06)_
- **R6** Light formula LABA02: sourceLight = clamp(baseline×cycle + Σ lamp, 0, 1); effective = clamp(source×shade×roof×canopy, 0, 1). Lamps add BEFORE shading. Signal glow adds 0. The inspector shows the lamp contribution separately and its effective value equals derived.light exactly. With no lamp the result is bit-identical to today's. _(SPEC §4.4, §4.10; CR R19; D07 §06)_
- **R7** Lamp neighbourhoods are cached and recomputed in stage 2 only when a lamp's cell, on state or intensity changes, a barrier state changes, terrain changes or shade changes. _(SPEC §11.2, §3.2 row 2; D07 §06)_
- **R8** Controllers: at most 8 per dish, one per target device. A rule records: ruleId, region (observation mask; empty is invalid), metric (mean O2, mean effective light, mean warmth, total sugar C, total living biomass, count of one ancestor template), optional template, direction High or Low, enter and reset thresholds (High needs reset < enter, Low needs reset > enter, both in the metric's domain), hold 1/5/10 s (default 5), cooldown 10/30/60 s (default 30), target, action, enabled, lastSampleTick, conditionStartTick, armed, lastFireTick. Actions: reservoir On/Off, lamp On/Off, S09 or S10 On/Off, S03 Open/Closed. A rule starts enabled and unarmed until it sees a reset sample, unless Arm now is chosen. _(SPEC §11.3, §2.5; CT §12.9, §14; D07 §07)_
- **R9** Sampling runs once per simulated second after stage 10, at the tick where (tick+1) % 10 === 0 (publish.ts's boundary), in ascending ruleId. The hold timer starts at the first qualifying sample and is cleared by any non-qualifying sample. When the hold has elapsed the rule fires: the action is queued with targetTick = tick+1, never the measured tick, and the rule disarms. It rearms after the cooldown AND a reset sample. A missing or invalid measurement disables the rule with a stated reason. Paused edits never advance timers or fire rules. _(SPEC §3.2 row 10, §11.3; D07 §07, §15)_
- **R10** Ordering and manual override. Every command (manual, schedule, controller) takes a seq from commands.nextSeq and applies in (targetTick, seq) order. A manual change to an actuator cancels its queued automatic actions, pauses its controller and its future scheduled actuator actions, and shows 'Manual control — automation paused'. Resume is explicit, and missed actions are marked skipped, never replayed. A second controller on a controlled device is rejected. A schedule plus a controller on one device needs an explicit acknowledgement. Removing a device cancels its queued actions and disables the controllers that reference it. _(SPEC §11.4; D07 §08)_
- **R11** Audit trail. Each firing records metric, region, value, thresholds, hold, target, queued tick and the result (for example 'On, but Empty'). A rule that is not firing shows its waiting state: reset, hold, cooldown, disabled or paused. A 'controller firing' event is emitted, and the chart shows threshold lines and firing markers. _(SPEC §11.4, §12.3, §12.5; D07 §08)_
- **R12** Recipes. LABR01: 12 B01 nearest (64,64) within r 2; a reservoir at (64,64) holding 6 sugar C + 0.6 N at 0.02/s; paired. The steady arm turns the reservoir On; the pulse arm runs a SETUP-ONLY release-all command at tick 0. Both arms run 300 s and every one of the 6 C must be accounted for. LABR02: baseLight 0.05; 12 A01 within r 2; a lamp at the centre at 0.50, On only in arm B; 300 s. LABR03: LABR01's steady arm with O2 0.25, lid closed, a circle probe r 3 at (64,64), and the Low-O2 rule (enter ≤ 0.30, reset ≥ 0.45, hold 5, cooldown 30, Off, Arm now); 60 s. _(CT §9.5; D07 §12)_
- **R13** Saves: devices are saved with inventory and settings, and controller records are saved with their queued actions. Snapshot, undo and comparison restore all equipment and automation state. Replay needs the same version and command log. The validator refuses unknown devices, missing masks, impossible ratios and duplicate ownership. _(SPEC §14.1; D07 §15)_
- **R14** Phase 6 fixtures: reservoir conservation; rate and empty (6 C empties after exactly 300 active seconds at 0.02/s); light formula (two lamps add before shading, closed shutters block, glow adds 0, the inspector equals photosynthesis); controller timing; manual precedence; persistence (1×, 4× and save/reload agree, including pending triggers). _(SPEC §15 test list (Phase 6); D07 §16)_

### Risks
- Controller mask source. Probes are unsaved worker session state (src/worker/probes.ts header). If a controller pointed at a probe id, reloading would change its measurement. The controller must store the mask by value, and the mask code must move into src/sim.
- Replay seq divergence. A controller takes its seq at stage 10. A player's applyNow at the next boundary takes a later seq, while a tool that pre-queues commands assigns different ones. nextSeq and the pending seq values are hashed. The manual-override pre-pass makes same-tick actuator conflicts independent of order. Fixtures must still replay through the host path.
- applyNow runs stageCommands, so a queued controllerAction due at the boundary is applied by the player's paused edit, not by the next step. The outcome is the same, but the manual-override cancellation has to look at that due set, not only at pending.
- Hold counting is off by one if the first qualifying sample counts as 1 s. LABR03's expected fire tick (59 or 49) depends on the ruling.
- Float identity. lightNeutral and lightFactors' fast path must both gain the LAB02 check, or the inspector and photosynthesis diverge. The lamp sum must accumulate in device (cell) order into one array, then add once to baseline×cycle.
- Lamp cache staleness. Device on/intensity edits replace the store but key on content (the roofCells lesson, wave 9 verifier fix). A move bumps geometryVersion, but a cache keyed only on that would miss on/intensity changes.
- Ledger semantics. The orchestrator's text says 'release is an input'. The canonical rule is that load is the input, release is internal, and empty or remove is an export. Ledgering the release would double-count 6 C in LABR01.
- Device-kind index codes. LAB01 and LAB02 must be appended after S12, or edgePassage KIND_SHELTER/KIND_MESH and the preview model shift.
- Interventions and challenge budgets. applyCommand increments pendingInterventions and stories counts every accepted command as a change. Controller firings must be excluded, or charts mark automation as player interventions.
- Blocked is practically unreachable, because structure edits refuse cells that hold a device. Implement it defensively (cell outside the dish or a structure present, e.g. from an import) and test it on a constructed world.
- Broth needs 'enzymes' fields. A broth load in a world without that system must be refused at stage 1 and at import.
- Commands, the content schema and recipes are contended across reservoir, lamp and controllers. The foundation task has to land every union member and schema field first.

### Open questions
- **Q:** Where does a controller's region come from, given that probes are unsaved worker state? **Options:** (a) the controller stores the mask by value, labelled with the probe letter; (b) make the six probe masks saved world state (a schema 6 store, hashed when non-empty); (c) rule ids reference probes and a reload rebuilds them **Recommendation:** (a). It is deterministic and self-contained, and a recipe can author it inline (LABR03). The UI offers to update the controller when the player edits the probe. Revisit (b) if P6.5 research records need saved regions.
- **Q:** Hold semantics: with 1 Hz samples, does hold 5 fire on the 5th or the 6th consecutive qualifying sample? **Options:** elapsed = sampleTick − conditionStartTick ≥ hold×10 (6th sample) | count of qualifying samples ≥ hold (5th) **Recommendation:** Elapsed time (D07 §07: 'elapsed qualifying time reaches the hold duration'; the first sample has elapsed 0). LABR03 fires at the tick-59 sample and Off applies at tick 60.
- **Q:** Which cells does the lamp path cross and light, and does an S04 channel edge transmit light? **Options:** transportOpen (water/gel/sediment plus bead) vs. ST_NONE only; S04 transmits vs. blocks **Recommendation:** Use transportOpen in the dish, with stone and wall blocking. Only a closed S03 blocks, so S01, S02, an open S03 and S04 transmit (D07: 'do not infer optical properties from transport class'). Owner flag.
- **Q:** Which cells count as 'valid habitat' for metric means? **Options:** every dish cell in the mask (as probes do) vs. transportOpen dish cells **Recommendation:** transportOpen dish cells (D07 §07 'valid habitat cells'). Structure cells hold no inventory and would dilute means.
- **Q:** How does stage 1 recognise a scheduled actuator action, so manual override can skip it? **Options:** the commandId prefix 'schedule:' (recipes.ts:638) vs. a new Command.origin field **Recommendation:** Use the prefix now; there is no schema change and old logs stay byte-identical. Add origin only if the player gains a schedule editor.
- **Q:** Is the setup-only release-all command ever player-facing? **Options:** recipe and experiment setup only vs. also a paused Lab tool **Recommendation:** Setup only. The host refuses it from UI messages and the experiment validator allows it. It is logged and creates no stock (D07 §12).
- **Q:** Is there one equipment flip, or one per LL stage? **Options:** one flip after P6.1–P6.3 vs. three **Recommendation:** One lead flip after all three are verified. Nothing shipped uses them earlier, and one flip means one fence record (D-0104 pattern).

## Slice: transfer-validation-release

### Summary

Verdict on LL4: enable it. A two-save transfer therefore recovers to both-old or both-new at every tested crash point. The one stated limitation is the one every single save already carries (ARCH §11.1): a power cut is outside the tested points because writeFile does not fsync. This is an owner item.

### Requirements
- **LL4-1** Cross-dish transfer needs both dishes paused and a compatible ruleset. Validate the whole destination first; any failure moves nothing, keeps the sample intact and keeps the transaction paused. It is a move, never a copy: fields keep cell offsets, organisms keep relative positions, attachment, every life state, genome, energy, age, infection and ownership unit. _(SPEC §10.5; D07 §09 'Living Lab transaction amendment' and 'Relationships and topology' (source only; SPEC §10.5 is the canonical summary); CR R18; D-0037)_
- **LL4-2** Commit the source export and the destination input together with one transaction id, and persist both saves in one atomic commit. Durable prepare and commit records exist for both saves. Recovery gives both-old or both-new, never one of each. A retry with the same txId can never duplicate inventory or organisms. _(SPEC §10.5, §14.1 ('sample slot and pending transaction'); D07 §15 'Data additions'; ARCH §11.1 as built (StorageBackend.commit))_
- **LL4-3** Cross-dish undo restores both worlds atomically, and only while neither world has changed since the transfer. One-sided undo is never offered: a per-dish Undo or rollback across the transfer is refused. _(SPEC §10.5; D07 §09)_
- **LL4-4** On reload, a pending transaction offers Complete, Cancel or Discard and never restarts biological time. Source lineage and intervention history stay visible after the transfer. A ruleset conversion is a separate copy operation done before the move. _(SPEC §10.5, §10.9, §14.5; D07 §09 'Why this matters to play')_
- **LL4-5** If atomicity cannot be guaranteed, keep cross-dish transfer disabled and report it as a limitation; same-dish transfer stays available. Proposed: enable it, and state the power-cut limit in EXPANSION_RESPONSE and the Recovery report. _(BUILD_DIRECTIVE P6.4; D07 §15, §17 'LL4 Safe transfer')_
- **LL4-6** Ledger: the source records an export and the destination an input, both tagged with the txId. Material totals are equal. Held samples count in world totals. _(D07 §15 'Material ownership'; src/sim/ledger.ts recordInput/recordExport)_
- **LL6-1** The D07 §16 table, with each row as a fixture. Reservoir conservation: starting stock = released + remainder + logged exports. Rate and empty: 6 C at 0.02/s empties after 300 active s, pauses and blocked ticks release 0, and the last tick transfers the exact remainder. Light formula: lamps add before shading, closed shutters block, glow gives no light, and the inspector agrees with photosynthesis. Controller timing: hold, hysteresis and cooldown; the action lands on the next tick, never in its own snapshot. Manual precedence: queued actions are cancelled, automation pauses, and resume does not replay. Sample transaction: failure moves nothing, cancel is exact, relationships stay valid, and recovery never duplicates. Persistence: 1×, 4× and save/reload agree, including pending triggers and samples. Access: no stamp changes biology, and an import cannot forge evidence. Understanding: a reviewer explains stock, light, one rule state, and a badge versus a trait. _(docs/source/D07…md lines 287–308 (canonical equivalent: the Phase 6 list in SPEC §17; SPEC §11.1–11.4))_
- **LL6-2** Save migration: v1.0 (rc1 corpus) and v1.1 (a new corpus) saves open in v1.2 with their recorded ruleset and recorded hashes, migrate by copy, and gain no equipment. A copy upgraded to Living Lab starts with no LAB01 or LAB02, no controllers and zero lamp contribution. _(SPEC §14.5; D07 §02 'Version and authority'; D-0035, D-0101, D-0102)_
- **LL6-3** Dense scene: 6,000 agents (2,000 fungal) with 256 devices (furnishings, S-devices and LAB01/LAB02 under the one cap), 512 edges, six regions and 8 controllers, run as the 15-minute stress session. Record real device count, effective speed, tick p50/p95, peak memory and touch p95. No controller evaluation, tick or transfer may be dropped. _(SPEC §16; D07 §15 'Bounded work', §16 'A small tuning review'; ARCH §13; D-0106 (shared 256 cap))_
- **LL6-4** Tuning: run LABR01–LABR03 at their stated seed, then LABR01 and LABR02 at the six development seeds. Report every outcome, extinction and no-difference included. Formative review target: 4 of 5 reviewers can explain an empty On feeder. _(D07 §16 'A small tuning review', 'Design review target')_
- **LL6-5** EXPANSION_RESPONSE gets a Living Lab section, with each feature marked Implemented, Partial, Deferred or Blocked and its build named. It also carries the Recovery report (undo, pending triggers, invalid references, interrupted transfers, migration, unsupported capability) and the Correctness report (the §16 table). _(D07 §18; CR R07; BUILD_DIRECTIVE Appendix A)_

### Risks
- Predecessor fallback can break both-or-neither. SaveStore.load falls back to the previous record when the current one is damaged. If one side of a committed pair is damaged, the store would load a pre-transfer source (still holding the sample) beside a post-transfer destination, or the other way round, which duplicates or loses material. The pair needs a guard: a slot whose current record belongs to a committed txId never falls back silently. Instead the player gets 'restore both to before the transfer?', applied as one paired commit.
- BirthIds collide across worlds. SPEC §10.5 says birthIds are unchanged only within the same dish (sample.ts:27). Cross-dish must issue new birthIds and remap every *BirthId column, including trap/heldBy, prey, host/parasite, and fungal and adhesion pairs. A missed column leaves a dangling reference, which phase5State's mutual-pair checks then refuse on the next load.
- Absolute-tick columns (lastIntakeTick, propTick, and any condition start or state timer stored as a tick) need rebasing to the destination clock; otherwise ages and E04/E12 timers jump. The full list has to come from ENTITY_COLUMNS, not memory.
- Genome and species indices are per world. Remap the genome table, including birth-proposal genome indices, with canonical dedupe, and remap species by id. A different index order must not change the meaning of any RNG key.
- Replay and determinism: the destination's command log must hold the full import payload, or rollback and replay of that dish cannot be reproduced. That payload can reach MBs at radius 6 with dense life. Check the command-log size and the 25 MB import cap against the dense case.
- Two tabs on the web: a pair commit without an expectSlots precondition could overwrite a slot that another tab saved meanwhile. The shelf's writer token does not cover named slots.
- A dest dish only in memory (unsaved new dish, comparison arm, Try my dish turn, demo) must be refused as a destination. Otherwise only one side is durable.
- IMPLEMENTED_SYSTEMS does not exist yet: implemented.ts lists none, and D-0119 places it at the systems flip. If v1.1.0 ships without it, a crafted schema-5 file naming 'equipment' could load in v1.1 with that system silently inert.
- The v1.1 corpus must be written before the Phase 6 buildPhase bump. If it is skipped, Phase 6 schema and light-formula edits have no file-level proof that real v1.1 saves keep their hashes; the trajectory fence covers recipes, not Phase 5 save state.
- Dense-scene cost: controllers toggling shutters, lamps and heaters every cooldown invalidate the lamp neighbourhood and barrier caches (geometryVersion). Measure the worst case at 8 controllers × 10 s cooldown; on overload reduce cosmetics only (SPEC §16).
- Rule gating: LAB02's term must leave stage 2's light arithmetic bit-identical where no lamp exists, or the fence and every corpus move.

### Open questions
- **Q:** Enable cross-dish transfer given that a power cut (not an app kill) is outside the tested crash points, since FilesystemBackend's writeFile does not fsync? **Options:** (a) Enable, and state the power-cut limit, the same limit every single save already carries. (b) Keep it disabled on Android and enabled on the web (IDB transaction). **Recommendation:** (a), with an owner flag. D07 asks for the guarantee the platform's tested commit gives, and the single-save path already accepts the same limit (ARCH §11.1).
- **Q:** What counts as a 'compatible ruleset' for a cross-dish move? **Options:** (a) Identical contentHash. (b) Equal evolutionRulesVersion, moduleRegistryVersion and phenotypeMappingVersion, with every carried species, module, system, structure and material recorded in the destination with an identical definition. **Recommendation:** (b). Under (a), a v1.0-recorded dish could never receive from a v1.2 dish even when the specimens' rules are identical, and the honest test is whether every carried genome keeps its meaning.
- **Q:** Does v1.2 offer the 'Living Lab copy' conversion (SPEC §10.9/§14.5, D07 §02) that D-0106 deferred for v1.1? **Options:** (a) Offer an equipment-only conversion: a new dish with 'equipment' and livingLabVersion 1 added, everything else unchanged, recorded as a provenance copy. (b) Keep deferring: equipment only in new dishes. **Recommendation:** (a). The equipment system is inert until a device is placed, the copy keeps the original, and it is the 'separate copy operation before moving the sample' that D07 §09 names for ruleset conversion. Owner flag.
- **Q:** Where does the import payload live for replay? **Options:** (a) Full payload in the destination's command log, and a checksum plus txId in the source's. (b) Payload in both. **Recommendation:** (a). The destination needs the payload for rollback and replay; the source's export is reproducible from its own held sample.
- **Q:** How is a transferred organism's lineage shown in the destination? **Options:** (a) A new root marked 'arrived from <dish> #<sourceBirthId> by transfer', with the source family history kept as display-only provenance. (b) Import the source lineage records. **Recommendation:** (a). Honest, bounded, and the destination's branch records never claim events it did not simulate.
