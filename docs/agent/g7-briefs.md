### Risks
- Stage stored in lifeState would break every 'lifeState≠0 = not Active' test (movement L456, births L76/L90, maintenance, glow) and lose E14's saved stage across dormancy. Use a separate lifeStage column.
- Applying stage factors outside profileOf would make inspector and sim diverge; they must go through the stage-keyed cache.
- Float identity: the ancestral score expression and the decide() candidate loop must stay textually the same for strategy 'ancestral'; put the strategy weights in a separate branch.
- File contention: movement.ts, intake.ts, births.ts, barriers.ts and structures.ts are touched by g5 waves 12–13 (predation, handling, traps, jacket speed), g6 (lamp, cross-sim) and P7.3 size (speed/contact) and P7.4 (E16 'no self-propulsion', roles). Schedule one owner per file per wave.
- A moving fungal entity breaks assumptions in fungi.ts (occupied, fungalPlacement, fungalNetwork), fungalTransport and the fungal connection-mask renderer, which assume a segment sits in its cell. Exclusion must be complete, or a disperser could join transport or block growth.
- The F02 parent reference: slot/birthId goes stale when the parent divides again (rekeying needs an O(n) scan). The entityId is stable but is per world, so Phase 6 cross-dish transfer and Sample must clear stageParent.
- The prey 'free' rule reads species.attached (movement.ts L159). Unchanged, it would make dispersing B02 uncatchable and E13 adults catchable as free prey.
- Night forager reads derived.light; paused commands use lastStageLightTick (climate.ts). The inspector's quiet/active must come from the column, not a re-read.
- The uniform developmental operation list spans three slices. Enabling developmental before P7.3/P7.4 operations exist would change draws again later, so there is one flip only.
- Decision-trace columns (decisionInput/Value) add saved state per organism in developmental worlds. That is fine for the hash, since flips move digests anyway, but the import must check their ranges.
- Perf: the U (4-neighbour) and T terms only for strategy organisms; measure dense scenes before and after (SPEC §16); never skip decisions.

### Risks
- Interning a founder or reference genome to draw a portrait would mutate the hashed genome table. Portrait derivation must use deriveProfile on a detached Genome. The world.profiles cache is derived and unhashed, which is acceptable only while deriveProfile stays pure.
- Adding sizeLocus to qualifies()'s mean dilutes the mean for old worlds and moves their branches. Every A09 criterion must be gated on the recorded evolutionRulesVersion, and neutral dev fields alone are not a safe gate.
- Two developmental switches exist: manifest developmentalEnabled (read by mutation.ts) and SYSTEM_FLAGS 'developmental' (checked by IMPLEMENTED_SYSTEMS). If they diverge, one could be on while the import check passes. Validator and import must require them to be equal.
- The import does not yet check developmentalEnabled, evolutionRulesVersion or phenotypeMappingVersion against the build. This must be fixed before v1.1.0 and v1.2.0 ship, not only in v1.3.
- D506 exactness: '0.20 C after 10 s' needs release from tick 1, so the pre-placed cache needs releaseEligibleTick ≤ the first tick. 'From the next tick after deposition' applies to deposits, not setup. Float accumulation over 100 × 0.002 must meet a stated tolerance (1e-9).
- D501 dwell cadence: if threshold timers run on the 0.5 s decision schedule, the switch lands in 15.0–15.5 s rather than at tick 150. The P7.1 owner fixes this and the fixture asserts the window.
- The current lineage follow re-targets silently to the nearest member when the followed member dies (renderer.ts ~1504), and a touch cancels follow. Both conflict with D05 (suspend and resume; no jump on extinction) and with existing e2e selectors (stop-following, following-label).
- The timeline store grows without bound unless capped. CT §12.10 has no timeline cap, so card count and compaction need a D-number, plus a size check against the 25 MB import cap.
- Snapshot availability lives on the device (checkpoint ring, shelf), not in the world. A stored 'replayable' flag can go stale as the ring rotates, so it must be verified at view time.
- After the Phase 6 cross-dish transfer, a lineage that 'arrived from' another dish has no local history. Portrait founder and timeline must say so and never claim the source dish's history.
- The four recordings must not be manufactured. 'A functional trait disappearing' may need an Accelerated run found by sim:tune or a labelled pre-seeded genome, and the label must say which.
- After the flip, the fence moves in every Standard recipe once the 1 % developmental draw fires. One flip keeps it to one re-record with proof by registryWith(old versions).
