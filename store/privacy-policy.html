Pixelmeba privacy policy

  • Effective date: 2026-10-12 (app version 1.0.0)
  • Developer: Lucid Winds Arcade
  • Contact: <owner: support email>

Pixelmeba is a single-player, offline game about a fictional pixel ecosystem. This policy covers the Android app (com.lucidwinds.pixelmeba) and the web version on the Lucid Winds Arcade.

The short version

Pixelmeba collects no data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking. Your dishes are saved on your device. A file leaves your device only when you choose Share or Export.

What Pixelmeba collects

Nothing. Pixelmeba does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or device information. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any account. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting software, and no third-party software development kit (SDK) that sends data anywhere.

What stays on your device

  • Your dishes and saves. Saved dishes, the automatic save, checkpoints, and your Notebook (favorites, nicknames, story cards, bookmarks, gallery entries, challenges, notes and goal progress) are kept in the app's own private storage on your device. On Android this is the app's internal data folder; on the web it is your browser's storage for the arcade page.
  • Your settings. Text size, sound, reduced motion and other choices are kept in the same private storage.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of this. Export a dish as a .pixelmeba file first if you want to keep a copy.
  • Android backup. The app allows Android's own backup (allowBackup). If you have turned on backup for your phone, Android itself may include Pixelmeba's saves in your Google account backup and restore them on a new phone. That backup is run by Android under your Google account settings, not by Pixelmeba; Pixelmeba sends nothing itself, and you can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

When something leaves your device: only when you choose

  • Share (a picture, a starting recipe or a living dish file) and Export (a .pixelmeba file) create a file and hand it to your device's system share sheet (on the web, a download). You choose where it goes, for example Files, Drive, email or a messaging app; that app's own privacy policy then applies. Cancelling sends nothing.
  • The file contains only what the Share sheet describes. A picture is an image of the dish (its text labels can be hidden, and it carries no device or location metadata). A starting recipe is the dish's setup and seed. A living dish file is the full save of that dish, which can include the names and notes you gave it; the Share and Export options let you leave them out ("Export without names or notes").
  • Import reads a file you pick; nothing is uploaded.

Permissions (Android)

  • INTERNET: declared by the app framework (Capacitor), which serves the game's own bundled files inside the app. Pixelmeba makes no network requests and needs no connection. (The permission stays until it has been tested on a device without it; see the developer's release checklist.)
  • VIBRATE: a short buzz when you place something, only if you turn Haptics on in Settings.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or notification permissions are requested.

Software inside the app

Pixelmeba is built with Capacitor. The Android app includes these Capacitor plugins, all running only on your device: App (pause and resume), Filesystem (saves in the app's private folder; shared files in its cache), Preferences (on-device key-value storage), Share (the system share sheet), Splash Screen and Status Bar. None of them transmits data.

Children

Pixelmeba collects no data from anyone, including children. It has no chat, no user accounts, no online features, no ads and no purchases inside the app.
